## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

模型一次前向计算能看到的全部 token，不只是用户说的那句话。

### Level 2 — 机制

每一类信息都占用窗口，都可能带来噪声或泄露数据。工具定义和工具结果常常比用户消息还长。

### Level 3 — 工程

Context Engineering 的工作，是决定这一步放什么、按什么顺序放、给多少预算。这比「写一个更好的 prompt」范围大得多。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Every token the model can see in one forward pass — not just the user's message.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Every kind of information takes up window space, and every kind can add noise or leak data. Tool definitions and tool results are often longer than the user message itself.

### Level 3 — Engineering

The job of context engineering is deciding what goes in at this step, in what order, and with what budget. That's a much bigger job than "write a better prompt."
