## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

在尽量少丢关键信息的前提下减少 token。

### Level 2 — 机制

可以删掉旧的对话轮次、给历史做摘要、截断工具输出、只保留结构化的事实。滚动摘要会随着对话不断更新。

### Level 3 — 工程

摘要本身会丢掉决策和错误信息。对于 coding agent 和长时间运行的 agent，要用 [Compaction](/docs/ai-native/compaction) 里的保留清单，而不是「把聊天记录总结一下」。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Cutting tokens while losing as little critical information as possible.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Drop old turns, summarize history, truncate tool output, keep only structured facts. A rolling summary gets updated as the conversation continues.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Summaries themselves drop decisions and errors. For coding agents and long-running agents, use the keep-list from [Compaction](/docs/ai-native/compaction) instead of "summarize the chat."

## Interview questions

2. How do you compress tool results without breaking the next step?
3. What are the failure modes of a rolling summary?
4. Are compression and retrieval alternatives, or do you combine them?
