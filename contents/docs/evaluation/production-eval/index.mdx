## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

把线上的失败变成离线的回归用例，形成闭环。

### Level 2 — 机制

Trace 记录 prompt 版本、检索内容、工具调用和输出。失败样本经过人工或评审模型标注后，进入 golden set。

### Level 3 — 工程

没有这个闭环，评测会和真实用户脱节。预发环境要用生产流量的影子流量或回放。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Turning production failures into offline regression tests, closing the loop.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Traces record the prompt version, retrieval, tool calls, and output. Failed samples get labeled by a human or a judge and go into the golden set.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Without this loop, evaluation drifts away from real users. Staging should use shadow or replayed production traffic.
