## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

Transformer 用 self-attention 建模序列里的依赖关系。现代 LLM 几乎都是 decoder-only。

### Level 2 — 机制

每个 block 里：attention 负责在 token 之间混合信息，FFN 在每个位置上做非线性变换，residual + norm 让很深的网络也能训得动。最后一层线性层输出 logits，再从中采样。

### Level 3 — 工程

面试时要能指出：位置编码影响长上下文能力；decoder-only 要靠 causal mask 才能自回归生成。细节数学放到 Attention / Training 页。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

A Transformer models dependencies across a sequence with self-attention. Almost every modern LLM is decoder-only.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Inside each block, attention mixes information across tokens and the FFN applies a per-position non-linearity. Residual connections plus normalization keep a deep stack trainable. A final linear layer produces logits, and the next token is sampled from them.

### Level 3 — Engineering

The points to land in an interview: positional encoding shapes how well the model handles long context, and a decoder-only model needs a causal mask to generate autoregressively. Leave the detailed math to the Attention and Training pages.
