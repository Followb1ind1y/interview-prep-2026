## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

预训练就是大规模的下一个 token 预测，让模型学到语言和世界知识的统计结构。

### Level 2 — 机制

每个位置都预测下一个 token，loss 是交叉熵。数据、模型规模和算力按 scaling laws 一起增长。

### Level 3 — 工程

面试时不要展开优化器论文。能讲清这一点就够：预训练给的是通用先验；完成任务的能力和「听话」来自 post-training。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Pretraining is next-token prediction at massive scale. It teaches the model the statistical structure of language and world knowledge.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Every position predicts the next token, and the loss is cross entropy. Data, model size, and compute are scaled up together along the scaling laws.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Don't wander into optimizer papers in an interview. The point to land: pretraining gives you a general prior; task skill and instruction following come from post-training.

## Interview questions

1. What objective is pretraining actually optimizing?
2. Why train with cross entropy instead of generating whole sentences directly?
3. How do scaling laws help with choosing a model and estimating quality?
4. The model's pretraining knowledge is out of date — do you fine-tune or add RAG?
