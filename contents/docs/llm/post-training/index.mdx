## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

Post-training 把「会续写」的模型，变成「会按指令做事、回答符合人类偏好」的模型。

### Level 2 — 机制

SFT 教格式和任务。RLHF 用 reward model + 强化学习；DPO 直接在偏好对上优化，省掉显式的奖励模型和采样。

### Level 3 — 工程

数据质量和偏好覆盖面决定模型行为。微调过猛会导致灾难性遗忘。生产环境里多数能力靠 RAG / prompt 解决，而不是把基座模型再训一遍。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Post-training turns a model that can continue text into one that follows instructions and gives the answers people prefer.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

SFT teaches format and tasks. RLHF uses a reward model plus reinforcement learning; DPO optimizes directly on preference pairs, skipping the explicit reward model and sampling loop.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Data quality and preference coverage decide how the model behaves. Fine-tune too aggressively and you get catastrophic forgetting. In production, most capabilities come from RAG or prompting — not from retraining the base model.
