## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

Agent 把不可信的内容和可执行的工具连在了一起，攻击面比普通 chatbot 大得多。

### Level 2 — 机制

间接注入藏在检索到的文档或网页里。模型一旦照做，就会滥用工具。隔离要靠权限、沙箱和人工确认。

### Level 3 — 工程

最小权限、高风险动作要确认、隔离文件系统和网络、不要把密钥放进 prompt。接到 [Sandbox](/docs/ai-native/sandbox) 和 [Context Isolation](/docs/context/isolation)。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

An agent connects untrusted content to tools that can take real actions, so its attack surface is much larger than a plain chatbot's.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Indirect injection hides inside retrieved documents or web pages. Once the model follows those instructions, it misuses its tools. Containment comes from permissions, sandboxes, and human confirmation.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Least privilege, confirmation for high-risk actions, isolated filesystem and network, and no secrets in the prompt. This connects to [Sandbox](/docs/ai-native/sandbox) and [Context Isolation](/docs/context/isolation).
