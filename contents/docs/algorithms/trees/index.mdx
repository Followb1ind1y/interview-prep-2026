## 模式

- 二叉树：用递归来定义，比写循环更接近面试时的表达
- BFS 层序遍历：队列 + 记录当前层的长度
- 图：先问清 **有向还是无向、有权还是无权、有没有环**

## Patterns

- Binary trees: a recursive definition is closer to how you'd explain it in an interview than a loop
- BFS level order: a queue plus the size of the current level
- Graphs: first ask **directed or undirected, weighted or unweighted, cyclic or not**
