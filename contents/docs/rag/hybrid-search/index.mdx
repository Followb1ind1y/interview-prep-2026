## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

同时走词面和语义两路检索，再把结果融合排序。

### Level 2 — 机制

加权融合需要先把两路的分数尺度校准。RRF 按名次融合，基本不用调参，结果更稳定。

### Level 3 — 工程

两路检索都要支持元数据过滤。融合之后再做 rerank。延迟是「两路并行 + 融合」，而不是简单地「多做一次检索」。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Running lexical and semantic retrieval side by side, then fusing the two rankings.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Weighted fusion needs the two score scales calibrated first. RRF fuses by rank instead, so there's almost nothing to tune and results are more stable.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Both paths need to support metadata filters. Rerank after fusion. The latency cost is two parallel retrievals plus fusion — not simply "one more search."
