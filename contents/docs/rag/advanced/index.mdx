## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

在基础 RAG 之上，在 query 侧或图结构侧加入推理，用来处理改写、多跳和实体关系。

### Level 2 — 机制

HyDE 先生成一个假设答案，再拿它去做 embedding 检索。多跳检索把中间答案变成下一跳的查询。GraphRAG 用实体关系弥补向量检索只看局部的问题。Agentic RAG 让模型自己决定要不要再搜一次。

### Level 3 — 工程

每多一跳，就多一份延迟和费用。先证明基础召回确实不够，再加组件。用评测证明查询改写真的有用。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Adding reasoning on the query side or through graph structure on top of basic RAG, to handle rewriting, multi-hop questions, and entity relationships.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

HyDE generates a hypothetical answer first and embeds that for retrieval. Multi-hop retrieval turns an intermediate answer into the next query. GraphRAG uses entity relationships to cover the local-only view of vector search. Agentic RAG lets the model decide whether to search again.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Every extra hop adds latency and cost. Prove that basic recall really falls short before adding components, and use evals to prove the rewrite step actually helps.
