## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

把文本变成稠密向量，语义相近的文本在向量空间里也靠得近。

### Level 2 — 机制

余弦相似度只看方向；点积会受向量长度影响，所以常先做归一化。Query 和文档有时会用非对称的模型分别编码。

### Level 3 — 工程

换 embedding 模型就要重建索引。维度越高不一定越好，但一定更贵。领域术语多、通用模型效果差时，用领域模型或 hybrid 检索。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Turning text into dense vectors so that semantically similar text ends up close together in vector space.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Cosine similarity only looks at direction; dot product is affected by vector length, so vectors are usually normalized first. Queries and documents are sometimes encoded by asymmetric models.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Switching embedding models means rebuilding the index. More dimensions isn't necessarily better, but it's always more expensive. When the domain vocabulary trips up a general model, use a domain model or hybrid search.
