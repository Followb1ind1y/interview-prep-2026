## 模板

- **S** 背景：谁、什么时候、为什么难
- **T** 你的职责——是「我」，不是「我们」
- **A** 你做的 3 个具体动作
- **R** 可量化的结果 + 你学到了什么

<Locale lang="en">

Every story should work at 2 minutes and compress down to 45 seconds. Pull them from the Tier 1 projects — don't invent a separate set of experiences that has nothing to do with the resume.

Interviewers love hearing: *It didn’t work initially. Here’s how I diagnosed it.*

## Template

- **S** Situation: who, when, and why it was hard
- **T** Task: your responsibility — "I", not "we"
- **A** Action: 3 concrete things you did
- **R** Result: a quantified outcome + what you learned
