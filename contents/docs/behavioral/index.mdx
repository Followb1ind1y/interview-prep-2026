## 必练题

- Tell me about yourself
- Why are you leaving?
- Why this company / this role?
- Biggest achievement / failure?
- Difficult technical decision?
- Conflict with teammate?
- Tight deadline?
- Production incident?
- A project that didn’t go well?

## 本专栏计划

先从 Tier 1 项目抽出 10 个 STAR（必须含失败），再练同一概念的两层讲解，最后进入 Mock 闭环。
