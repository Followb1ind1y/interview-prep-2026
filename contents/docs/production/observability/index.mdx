## 三级回答

### Level 1 — 定义

能事后复盘：一次请求为什么慢、为什么贵、为什么错。

### Level 2 — 机制

分布式 trace 把所有子调用串起来。Prompt 必须有版本号。检索到的文档 id 要能回放。

### Level 3 — 工程

日志里不要出现明文密钥和用户隐私。高流量时做采样，失败的请求全量保存。接到评测闭环。

## Three-level answers

### Level 1 — Definition

Being able to reconstruct why a single request was slow, expensive, or wrong.

### Level 2 — Mechanism

Distributed tracing ties all the sub-calls together. Prompts must carry a version number. Retrieved document IDs have to be replayable.

### Level 3 — Engineering

Keep plaintext secrets and user PII out of logs. Sample high-volume traffic, and keep every failed request. Feed it all into the eval loop.
